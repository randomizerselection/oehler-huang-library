Show realistic industrial proportions and refined tactile detail.

Warm amber light in the workshop, restrained cyan highlights on machinery, cinematic depth, beautiful editorial architectural rendering. The image should look impressive but calm, with one clear focal subject, not an infographic.
No text of any kind, no labels, no arrows, no charts, no floating coins, no keys, no policy mini-scenes, no collage, no multiple panels, no tax forms, no banknotes, no city skyline. Do not try to explain the whole lesson. Communicate one idea: a firm adding productive equipment. Wide 16:9 landscape.
