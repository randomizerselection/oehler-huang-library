Scope: the existing Lesson 4 (26 slides) and Lesson 5 (28 slides). The original
slide data, Cambridge questions, mark-scheme source records, study views and
lesson sequence are preserved.

- Causal stages now have meaningful line illustrations, continuous directional
  arrows, aligned bilingual explanations and a distinct final effect. Key-term
  answers retain their space before and after reveal.
- Comparisons have aligned English/Chinese headings and body baselines;
  sequences have connecting arrows. The time-lag layout uses a timeline, and
  current-account contributions lead to one resulting balance.
- Calculations use readable stacked fractions and prominent results, with the
  complete calculation retained as an accessible text alternative.

  spacing and palette. Original question wording and separate source controls
  remain visible. Presentation-only code is owned by these two lessons.
