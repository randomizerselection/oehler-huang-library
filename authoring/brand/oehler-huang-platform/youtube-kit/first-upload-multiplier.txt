TITLE
The Multiplier: Income–Expenditure Explained | A Level Economics

DESCRIPTION
How can £50 million of extra investment increase national income by £200 million? Follow the income–expenditure diagram to see the multiplier work step by step.

This lesson explains the upward shift in planned aggregate expenditure, the successive rounds of induced consumption, and the movement towards a new equilibrium.

Course: Cambridge A Level Economics 9708 — national income determination, 9.1.1.
Model: a closed economy without government, fixed prices, and spare capacity. MPC = 0.75; planned investment rises from £100m to £150m; equilibrium income rises from £400m to £600m.

PINNED COMMENT
Quick check: if MPC were 0.8, how much would equilibrium income rise after the same £50m increase in investment? Assume the same model conditions. Show your calculation.

TEACHER’S REPLY — keep separate from the question
k = 1 ÷ (1 − 0.8) = 5, so ΔY = 5 × £50m = £250m.
