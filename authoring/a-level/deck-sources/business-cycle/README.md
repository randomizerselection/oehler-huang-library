## Curriculum decision

The 2026–2028 syllabus p.30 explicitly puts phases, causes and automatic stabilisers
in 9.2.3, following actual/potential growth and output gaps. Bamford & Grant §42.3
(retained PDF p.362) follows that sequence. The multiplier and accelerator are
already taught 9.1 prerequisites: retrieve their causal links for cycle propagation,
without another derivation or fiscal-injection calculation lesson.

Introduce the distinction between stabilising actual output and increasing capacity
at the end; the next lesson develops 9.2.4 policy effectiveness. Then teach 9.2.5
inclusive growth and 9.2.6 sustainability. A synoptic essay's relevance does not
justify moving all of its prerequisite policy evaluation ahead of this sequence.
