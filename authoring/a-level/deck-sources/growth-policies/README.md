## Historical case evidence and photography

- Elizabeth line: [DfT, 24 May 2022](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cheaper-tickets-and-quicker-journeys-as-part-government-funded-elizabeth-line-officially-opens).
  Opening announcement gives +10% central London rail capacity and a reduction
  in Abbey Wood–Paddington journey time from 58 to 29 minutes. These are transport
  measures, not a measured 10% national GDP increase.
- Actual Bond Street platform photo: [Sunil060902, 25 October 2022](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bond_Street_stn_Elizabeth_line_25th_Oct_2022_05.jpg),
  CC BY-SA 4.0. Local original copy, cropped by CSS; attribution/licence in Sources.
- Singapore: [MOE 2024 announcements](https://cos.moe.gov.sg/2024-announcements/)
  records the S$4,000 SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) top-up for Singaporeans
  aged 40+ from 1 May 2024. Treat this as a policy input, not measured productivity.
- [ProjectManhattan’s ITE HQ photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aeroplane_at_ITE_HQ,_Singapore.jpg),
  2014, CC BY-SA 3.0, is existing course imagery. It supplies specifically relevant
  country/technical-education context, not evidence of 2024 participation.

All cases have small on-demand sources and fuller teacher notes. Data-driven
claims and teacher-inferred mechanisms are distinguished. No simulated index is
labelled as historical data.
