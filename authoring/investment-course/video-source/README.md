# SFLS Stock Market Game launch video

64-second bilingual classroom trailer for Suzhou Foreign Language School,
11 September 2026. Output: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4, stereo.
English and Simplified Chinese text are burned into the picture. Instrumental
music and transition sounds are synthesized by the builder; there is no narration.

Run `build_launch_video.py` with the bundled Python runtime. Dependencies: Pillow,
NumPy and imageio-ffmpeg. `--preview` produces a scene contact sheet only.
The authoring source is separate from the public lesson and does not change it.
Outputs are in `authoring/investment-course/outputs/stock-market-game-launch/`.
