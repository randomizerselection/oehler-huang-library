Existing SMG accounts do not establish permission to enter InvestWrite. Seek written confirmation covering this China-based class, the actual SMG session and any special international arrangement. No confirmation is on record in this task, and no enquiry or entry has been sent.

## Classroom implementation

- Introduce an optional writing project while making the eligibility position clear. If no exception is granted, students may still complete the school project, without presenting it as an official entry.
- Keep dated research and order records from the beginning. Ask each student what they expected, what happened, what they learned and what they would change. A loss, rejection or decision to wait can prompt learning, but an essay must still address the actual official prompt and investment experience.
- Use the November diversification and allocation lessons to support the long-term reasoning. Do not require December financial-statement or valuation teaching as prior knowledge for a November draft.
- Give feedback through questions about evidence, reasoning, completeness and clarity. Students must compose and revise their own prose. Do not supply a model essay for copying or generate their entry with AI.
- Review word count, English, attribution, prompt coverage, original authorship and eligible-session participation before any entry. Check current rules and the submission page again; do not assume a deadline time zone. Retain confirmation of any accepted submission.
- Assess the course through individual calculations and judgements, the team decision record and the final portfolio defence. Competition participation or placing is not a requirement for success in the course.
