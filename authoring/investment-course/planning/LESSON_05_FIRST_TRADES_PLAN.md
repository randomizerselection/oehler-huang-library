## Classroom exercise rules

These are proposed teaching rules for the first weekend, distinct from official SMG rules: use virtual cash only; 1–2 eligible ordinary stocks; buy-limit orders; no borrowing, shorting or leveraged/inverse products. Combined fee-inclusive commitment at most $20,000 and at most $10,000 per stock. Apply caps cumulatively across all filled and pending first-weekend orders. Preserve the remaining cash for subsequent portfolio lessons. Two stocks are an initial exercise, not a claim of sufficient diversification. A buy limit limits purchase price, not later losses. Do not raise limits merely to make a deadline.

Retain the established roles. Organiser / 组长 records the decision and submits the team report. Research lead / 研究负责人 combines everyone’s facts and risks. Portfolio coordinator / 账户负责人 checks account values, agreed tickets and subsequent execution. Everyone contributes and agrees; one agreed operator enters while another checks. Roles remain stable until the established checkpoint.

## Team record and QQ submission

One record per team, using these fields for each stock:

1. Company, ticker, exchange and business in one sentence / 公司、代码、交易所及一句话业务介绍。
2. Dated fact, reporting period and original source URL / 有日期的事实、报告期间和原始链接。
3. Why the fact matters, one risk, and what could change the decision / 事实意义、一项风险及改变决定的条件。
4. Quote with timestamp/currency; buy limit; whole shares; fee and maximum cost / 带时间和币种的报价、买入限价、整数股数、费用及最高成本。
5. Cash remaining after all commitments; members’ agreement / 预留所有订单资金后的剩余现金及全组同意。
6. Submission time, reference and exact status / 提交时间、编号及准确状态。

By Sunday 20 September at 20:00 China time, the organiser sends one QQ message and order/status screenshot through the existing teacher contact. Use a team identifier, hide passwords, and do not publish private records in the public repository. Include any access, eligibility or rejected-order problem by the same deadline. Do not duplicate a pending order because a holding is not yet visible.

By Tuesday 22 September at 20:00 China time, add actual fill quantity, price, fee, date and status, plus matching holdings or the rejection reason. Reconcile cash to actual cost and any separately posted account items. A weekend submission is not a weekend fill. General SMG rules price weekend real-time stock orders at the next business-day open, and end-of-day orders at the next close. If the teacher requires completed trades before Sunday, use a supported Friday 18 September evening window from 21:30 China time instead, subject to mode and posting delays. No overnight attendance is required.

Assess evidence, arithmetic, teamwork and accurate records rather than the first few days’ profit. Record actual slide coverage after Wednesday and Friday before further pacing changes.
