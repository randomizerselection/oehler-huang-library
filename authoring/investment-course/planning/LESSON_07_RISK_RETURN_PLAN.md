# Lesson 7: Risk and possible return

Prepared 23 September 2026 in response to the request for the next new Investment lesson after market capitalisation. Canonical source: `apps/library/investment-analysis/lessons/risk-possible-return/`. It is linked immediately after Share price and company size. The saved pre-exam revision slot and subsequent timetable remain unchanged; no additional teaching is recorded.

**40 minutes; 30 core slides + 2 optional.** The new assessed skill is translating a possible percentage-return range into ending values and downside losses, then distinguishing what an outcome range, an expectation and a price path tell us.

The deck carries forward the newest preferences: concept-led titles; clear syllabus hierarchy; three bilingual objectives; retrieval before new material; a directly relevant photograph and prediction; mechanism before definition; staged diagrams with reversible reveals; a connected Tesla case with explicit historical/scenario distinction; formula on the first calculation slide; full worked solutions and protected independent practice; MCQs with misconception-specific feedback; assessed individual exit before Summary; optional extensions afterwards.

Annualised/required return and nominal/real return are not prerequisites. Expected return is taught conceptually; no probability-weighted calculation or formal volatility statistic is introduced. Scenario endpoints are not represented as guaranteed limits. A past recovery is not a promise of another one. The source notes preserve exact conventions, links and image licensing.
