# Public site identity

The public site name is **学思札记**. Use it consistently on the Library,
course/resource pages, EconMark, account dialogs and Student Selector.

Keep personal names out of site-wide branding, general introductions and footers.
Retain genuine author biographies and lesson/source credits in their own context.
Do not identify a material author as the filing holder or site operator. Do not add the filing
holder's personal name to public pages. Keep author biography and source credits
distinct from site ownership; a school affiliation is an author credential, not
a claim that the school operates the site.

The site is described as a personal, noncommercial site with public economics
notes, actual classroom lessons and practice, teaching reflections, and homework
submission, storage and feedback tools. Do not disguise lessons as notes or omit
EconMark, automatic marking or account registration. General site copy and
navigation are Chinese-first; do not translate, remove or change the languages of
lesson materials for filing purposes.

EconMark is private by default (`OH_ECONMARK_PRIVATE=true`). Anonymous visitors
cannot retrieve its pages, JavaScript, sample assets, provider status or assignment
details, including through compatibility paths. Existing accounts remain usable;
new student registrations require a valid teacher-issued class invitation, and
new teacher registrations require an administrator invitation. Legacy open
registration is disabled in private mode. Do not claim all accounts belong to
the author’s students: existing accounts are preserved. Synthetic examples are
available inside the private tool. `OH_ECONMARK_PRIVATE=false` is reserved for
explicit local public-demo regression; keep production private.

The public scope statement says there is no enrolment, paid training or sale of
goods. Revisit this statement if the actual service changes. Do not hide features
temporarily for filing review or treat branding changes as proof of eligibility.

The private filing application must accurately identify the actual responsible
organizer and describe the site's services. Its purpose statement must not use
“本人教学” or imply that all materials were written by the applicant when that is
not true. Province-specific eligibility still needs confirmation with the filing
provider. Add the genuine approved ICP number and MIIT link after approval;
never invent a number or claim approval before it is granted.

Source: Alibaba Cloud, “填写网站信息”:
https://help.aliyun.com/zh/icp-filing/basic-icp-service/user-guide/fill-in-website-information
