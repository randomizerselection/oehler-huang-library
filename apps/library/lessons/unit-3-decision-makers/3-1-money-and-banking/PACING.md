## Preferences carried forward

- Characteristic-introduction questions accompanied by images showing the exact situation, with teaching cues in notes. The IC Buck discussion remains question-only.
- Exact, relevant photographs and the latest diamonds scenario.
- One characteristic at a time: illustrated question, photo-led bilingual definition with a keyword blank, then a quick formative check.
- Full-screen overview images after teaching and checking the content.
- One question-only IC Buck discussion using the department currency students already know; judge it from its actual design and rules, without inventing a model answer.
- Varied checks and original past-paper questions, followed by separate model answers grounded in the local mark schemes.
