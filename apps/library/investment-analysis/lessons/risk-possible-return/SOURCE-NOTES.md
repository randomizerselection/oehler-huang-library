# Risk and possible return

Prepared 23 September 2026. Lesson 7, syllabus 1.2.1; 40 minutes, 30 core slides and two optional applications after Summary. The scheduled pre-exam revision period remains intact. Preparation does not establish taught coverage.

## Evidence and data conventions

- [Tesla 2025 Form 10-K, Item 5, printed page 30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm): the Stock Performance Graph covers January 2021–December 2025, beginning with $100. The chart is historical cumulative total return, including the effect of stock splits. Tesla reports no cash dividends during this period. The deck's three-point visual uses approximately $50 at end-2022 and $190 at end-2025, read from the graph and deliberately labelled as rounded estimates. Lines connect selected dates; intermediate movements are omitted. Do not use the rounded readings as exact share quotations or forecasts.
- [Tesla-hosted original report](https://ir.tesla.com/_flysystem/s3/sec/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231-gen.pdf); [report mirror used for visual inspection](https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReports/PDF/NASDAQ_TSLA_2025.pdf). The original embedded chart is retained locally as `../../course-assets/images/risk-possible-return/tesla-performance.jpeg` and linked from the historical slide's Sources control. It is reproduced as source evidence, not presented as an original illustration or under the photo licences below.
- Tesla's 2025 filing also identifies competition, pricing, demand and margin risks. The competition → expected margin → bids → possible return chain is a conditional teaching interpretation, not a claim to explain a specific historical price change.
- [FINRA: Risk](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investing-basics/risk) and [SEC: Five questions before investing](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/investorpubsfivequestionshtm) support the distinction between possible reward and guaranteed return. Definitions and question wording are teacher-written.

All Emma/Lucy/Jack transactions, scenario percentages, range comparisons and paired paths are hypothetical. They exclude income, costs, taxes and external cash flows. Three scenarios are not equally likely by default, not a probability distribution, and not a complete set of possible outcomes. Expected return cannot be calculated from an unweighted list of scenarios. Volatility compares fluctuations over equal intervals; the core does not teach standard deviation or probability-weighted expected-return calculations.

## Teaching and assessment

Retrieve only shares and total return before new teaching. Do not retrieve annualised return, forecast assumptions or real return as taught knowledge. Expected and realised return are introduced from a concrete before/after case, then defined. The core new application is calculating ending values and downside losses from alternative one-period returns, and judging what ranges and paths can and cannot establish.

Protect 90 seconds for independent range calculation and four minutes for the final four MCQs before feedback. Two earlier hinge MCQs identify expectation/outcome and range/probability misconceptions. Every final MCQ is original teacher-written practice, worth one mark; question and options remain visible during feedback. Teacher notes include distractor diagnoses and a named visual to revisit. Optional recovery and expectation-shortfall questions follow Summary.

Pacing: retrieval/objectives 0–3; risk and expectations 3–13.5; outcome calculation and practice 13.5–23; volatility and trade-off 23–30.5; four independent MCQs 30.5–34.5; feedback 34.5–38; Summary and buffer 38–40.
