# First monthly exam review

Lesson 6 · 40 minutes · prepared 23 September 2026.

Revision of investment/saving/speculation, returns and cash flow, compound growth,
share orders, market capitalisation, stock splits and expectations. Original
practice questions and changed numerical examples are used throughout. All
company numbers introduced with “Suppose” are hypothetical, not market quotes
or actual company announcements. No future exam questions or answer key are
included in this public lesson.

The existing course lessons provide the teaching definitions and methods:

- [Measuring investment return](../1-1-2-measuring-investment-return/index.html)
- [Compound growth](../1-1-3-compound-growth/index.html), through rates and periods
- [Planning your first stock trades](../first-stock-trades/index.html)
- [Share price and company size](../share-price-company-size/index.html)

Assumed return, annualised return, nominal/real return, and the later risk lesson
are outside this revision. The independent check is original teacher-written
practice, not an official past paper. Formulae retain the course's full-word
return notation and FV = P(1 + r)^n.
