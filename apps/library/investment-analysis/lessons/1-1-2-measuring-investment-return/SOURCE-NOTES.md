- Formula source of truth: the same course map — `Total return amount = ending value − starting value + income`; `total return percentage = total return amount ÷ starting value × 100`.
- Chinese terminology follows the same course map where available: `capital gain · 资本利得`, `coupon · 票息`, `bond · 债券`, `share · 股票`, `dividend · 股息` and `total return · 总回报`. Lesson-specific support also uses `capital loss · 资本损失`, `REIT · 房地产投资信托基金` and `Treasury note · 美国中期国债`.
- The opening hook compares Apple and Coca-Cola over the same current 31 December 2025 to 31 July 2026 measurement window. The later A/B calculation case is deliberately constructed to isolate the difference between amount and percentage. All slides labelled with a company or asset use the dated evidence below.
- Terminology checked on 4 September 2026: the GIPS Standards Handbook defines external cash flows as capital entering or exiting a portfolio. Investor contributions/withdrawals are therefore labelled “external cash flow · 外部现金流”, not “new cash flow”; dividends are investment income, not investor contributions. https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/
- Link (领展) is the proper name of Link Real Estate Investment Trust, not a separate asset category. Its official description identifies it as a Hong Kong-listed REIT; the lesson explains the name and distinguishes units in the fund from directly owned physical property. https://www.linkreit.com/en/about-us/

## Real-world return evidence

- Apple January–July 2026: Nasdaq historical data records closes of US$271.86 on 31 December 2025 and US$308.91 on 31 July 2026. Apple paid US$0.26 per share on 12 February and US$0.27 on 14 May, so cash dividend income received during the period was US$0.53 per share. Capital gain was US$37.05, total return amount was US$37.58 and total return percentage was `(308.91 − 271.86 + 0.53) ÷ 271.86 × 100 = 13.8%` (rounded to one decimal place). https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/aapl/historical and https://investor.apple.com/dividend-history/
- Coca-Cola January–July 2026: Nasdaq historical data records closes of US$69.91 on 31 December 2025 and US$87.59 on 31 July 2026. Coca-Cola paid two US$0.53 quarterly dividends during the period, on 1 April and 1 July, for US$1.06 per share. Capital gain was US$17.68, total return amount was US$18.74 and total return percentage was `(87.59 − 69.91 + 1.06) ÷ 69.91 × 100 = 26.8%` (rounded). https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/ko/historical, https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/board-elects-new-officer-and-approves-annual-dividend-increase and https://www.coca-colacompany.com/media-center/board-of-directors-elects-two-new-officers-and-declares-regular-quarterly-dividend
- Apple 2022 negative-return practice: period-end closes were US$177.57 for 2021 and US$129.93 for 2022; Apple paid US$0.91 per share across the four 2022 dividends. `(129.93 − 177.57 + 0.91) ÷ 177.57 × 100 = −26.3%` (rounded). This older record is intentionally retained and labelled as a historical contrast because it demonstrates a company-share capital loss large enough to outweigh positive dividend income. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886982/000095017025051694/aapldi04_prelim.htm and https://investor.apple.com/dividend-history/
- U.S. Treasury note example: TreasuryDirect explains that notes pay a fixed rate of interest every six months and may be held to maturity or sold before maturity. The 10-year note auctioned on 11 February 2026 (CUSIP 91282CPZ8) has a 4.125% interest rate, was issued on 17 February 2026 and matures on 15 February 2036. At US$1,000 face value, the coupon is `1,000 × 4.125% = US$41.25` per year, paid as two US$20.625 instalments. https://treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/treasury-notes/ and https://www.treasurydirect.gov/instit/annceresult/press/preanre/2026/R_20260211_2.pdf
- Link REIT property example: Lok Fu Place is an actual Link retail, car-park and fresh-market property in Kowloon. For FY2025/26, Link’s closing unit price fell from HK$36.40 to HK$36.02, a HK$0.38 capital loss per unit, while distribution per unit was 253.61 Hong Kong cents (HK$2.5361). Total return amount was therefore HK$2.1561 per unit and total return percentage was `2.1561 ÷ 36.40 × 100 = 5.9%` (rounded). https://www.linkreit.com/en/business/properties/lok-fu-place/ and https://www.linkreit.com/-/media/linkreit/investor-relations/financial-data/financial-highlights/ar2526-five-year-performance-summary_en.pdf
- Gold example: the World Gold Council reports that the USD gold price was down 7.8% from the end of 2025 to 31 July 2026. Physical gold does not provide a regular cash flow such as a dividend or coupon; before holding costs, its total return therefore equals its −7.8% price change. https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-market-commentary-july-2026 and https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/relevance-of-gold-as-a-strategic-asset/risks-and-challenges
- Apple-versus-Coca-Cola comparison: the percentage returns use the same real January–July 2026 period, while the starting holdings are deliberately hypothetical so the amount and percentage rankings differ. `US$20,000 × 13.8% = US$2,760` for Apple; `US$10,000 × 26.8% = US$2,680` for Coca-Cola. The comparison excludes fees and tax.
