## Role clarification following teacher feedback

The [official Team Roles handout](https://www.stockmarketgame.org/document/Team_Roles.pdf), checked 11 September 2026, gives everyone a research contribution and opportunities to log in and enter trades, while a coordinator oversees correct entry and records. It does not impose three independent logins per meeting. The [2022 Advisor Guide](https://www.stockmarketgame.org/document/2022%20SMG%20Advisor%20Guide.pdf), Creating Teams/Team Setup and Assigning Roles, permits combined roles and optional rotation; its weekly example is not a compulsory schedule.

Our three-role adaptation combines Director with Lead Reporter (A), Head Researcher with a classroom risk-checking responsibility (B), and Portfolio Coordinator with Data Entry Coordinator (C). Keep teams and initial roles stable for the first half-term, then review at an announced checkpoint. Supported turns at the account provide broader learning without weekly changes of responsibility. This timetable and the one-off weekend access check are classroom recommendations. No extra research or trade is needed this weekend. One person may capture the screenshot; the organiser reports all login results, including an error or lack of a device, by the deadline. All members contribute evidence and decisions later; the coordinator does not gain authority to trade alone.

The simplified student labels are Organiser / 组长, Research lead / 研究负责人, and Portfolio coordinator / 账户负责人. The official-role mapping above remains in teacher guidance. Each student sees one main job, three short actions and an example; information gathering, risk checking and decisions stay shared.
