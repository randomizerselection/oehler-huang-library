Lesson-by-lesson planning for the macroeconomy, government intervention and international economic issues.

Pacing

Sections 9–10 retain the workbook’s 28¾ lesson units. Section 11 adds 19 provisional units from the earlier coverage planner. A quarter-lesson review at the end of section 9 brings the sequence to 48 lessons.

Planning assumptions and workbook sources

The complete plan takes 24 teaching weeks at two lessons per week. It exceeds the earlier 16-week, 32-lesson model by 16 lessons. It includes the quarter-lesson review above, but no separate assessment lessons or holiday allowance. Dates are left blank for your timetable.

Lesson shares preserve the workbook’s allocations in syllabus order. A shared lesson divides its time between the listed points. Teaching activities, checks and follow-up tasks are planning suggestions, not prescribed syllabus wording or teaching hours.
