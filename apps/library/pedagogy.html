Teaching approach

教学方式

Three courses share a commitment to clear reasoning. The emphasis changes: building economic explanations, connecting formal models, or interpreting financial decisions.

三门课程都重视清晰推理，但各有侧重：建立经济学解释、连接正式模型，或解读财务决策。

Learning purpose学习目标 Concepts → explanations. Use precise terms, explain cause and effect, and develop exam answers.

从概念走向解释：准确使用术语，说明因果关系，展开考试答案。

 Models → conditional conclusions. Connect equations, data and diagrams; explain when a predicted effect holds.

从模型走向有条件的结论：连接公式、数据与图表，说明预测成立的条件。

 Evidence → financial judgement. Calculate results, compare alternatives and state the limits of the conclusion.

从证据走向财务判断：计算结果、比较选择，并说明结论的局限。
Lesson opening如何导入 A familiar issue, photograph or recall task makes the concept concrete before formal explanation.

通过熟悉的问题、照片或回忆任务，使概念具体化，再进入正式解释。

 A macroeconomic puzzle or prior-model recall prepares students for a new relationship or mechanism.

以宏观经济问题或已有模型的回顾，为新关系、新机制作准备。

 A financial puzzle invites a prediction before the rule: who earned more, or why did interest grow?

先用财务问题引发预测，再介绍规则：谁赚得更多？利息为何增加？
How ideas are explained如何讲解 Short definitions, concrete examples, diagrams and visible causal steps. Keyword blanks focus attention on essential links.

使用简短定义、具体例子、图表与完整因果步骤；关键词填空突出关键联系。

 Move between verbal mechanisms, formulas, worked calculations, data tables and interactive diagrams.

在文字机制、公式、计算示例、数据表与交互图表之间建立联系。

 Introduce the term, show asset or company examples, then calculate step by step using dated evidence or labelled assumptions.

先介绍术语与资产或公司实例，再用注明日期的证据或明确假设逐步计算。
Student practice学生如何练习 Recall, classify, complete causal explanations, discuss with a partner and turn keywords into written answers.

回忆、分类、补全因果解释、同伴讨论，并把关键词组织成书面答案。

 Calculate, interpret diagram changes, distinguish similar mechanisms and apply analysis to exam questions.

完成计算、解读图表变化、区分相近机制，并将分析应用于考试题。

 Classify returns, calculate and compare cases, answer misconception checks, and build a Stock Market Game evidence record.

对回报分类、计算与比较案例、辨析常见误解，并建立模拟股票交易的证据记录。
Feedback & success反馈与达标表现 Answer reveals and model responses check each causal link. Success means a developed explanation, with balance and judgement when required.

通过答案揭示与参考答案检查因果环节；达标意味着解释充分，并在题目要求时兼顾双方、作出判断。

 Worked feedback and MCQs expose calculation and modelling errors. Success connects the correct result to a diagram and a relevant qualification.

通过计算反馈与选择题发现计算或模型误解；达标意味着连接正确结果、图表与相关限制条件。

 Check method, units and interpretation. Success includes the relevant period or assumptions, and distinguishes a projection from a promise.

检查方法、单位与解释；达标需要说明相关期间或假设，并区分预测与保证。
Language support语言支持 Frequent Chinese support for definitions and explanation steps; English keywords support written expression.

较多使用中文辅助定义与解释步骤，以英文关键词支持书面表达。

 Chinese support for technical concepts; English reasoning and consistent mathematical notation remain central.

以中文辅助专业概念，重点保持英文推理与一致的数学表达。

 English first, with Chinese for difficult financial terms. Short cases explain unfamiliar companies or assets.

以英文为主，中文辅助较难的金融术语；简短案例介绍不熟悉的公司或资产。
In the lessons课例与变化

Merit and demerit goods: a school drinks question leads to definitions and written explanation.

Cocoa and chocolate review: retry full questions from a paper extract.

有益品与有害品课从校园饮料问题进入概念与解释；可可与巧克力复习课从试卷材料出发，重做完整题目。

The multiplier: spending rounds, calculations, income diagrams and spare-capacity evaluation.

Components of aggregate demand: schedules, function shifts and the accelerator.

乘数课连接支出轮次、计算、收入图表与闲置产能评价；总需求课使用数据表、函数移动与加速数。

Measuring investment return: distinguish money earned from percentage return.

Compound growth: year-by-year reinvestment and an assumptions statement.

回报课区分赚取金额与回报率；复利课用逐年再投资计算，并要求写出假设条件。

02 / Shared foundations

What the courses share各课程的共同点

Try before feedback先尝试，再反馈

Predict, discuss or calculate before revealing the answer.

先预测、讨论或计算，再揭示答案。

Show the reasoning展示推理过程

Make the explanation, method and evidence visible.

清楚呈现解释、方法与证据。

Explain the conclusion说明结论的依据

Say why an answer follows and which conditions matter.

说明答案为何成立，以及哪些条件重要。

For parents面向家长 Ask for a definition, redrawn diagram or short explanation. Use the linked handouts, quizzes and flashcards for practice.

请学生回忆定义、重画图表或写简短解释；使用课件中的讲义、测验与闪卡练习。

 Ask students to redo a calculation, explain its diagram and name a condition that could change the conclusion.

请学生重新计算、解释相关图表，并指出可能改变结论的条件。

 Ask students to recalculate a case, explain the result in one sentence and state its period or assumptions.

请学生重新计算案例，用一句话解释结果，并说明期间或假设。
For teachers面向教师 Check vocabulary and each causal link before extending the written answer.

先检查术语和每个因果环节，再扩展书面答案。

 Check that students can connect words, equations and diagrams before asking for evaluation.

先确认学生能连接文字、公式与图表，再要求评价。

 Check financial meaning, measurement basis and assumptions alongside numerical accuracy.

检查数字准确性，同时检查财务含义、衡量口径与假设。
