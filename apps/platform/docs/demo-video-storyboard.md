# Three-minute competition video storyboard

| Time | Picture | Narration point |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Teacher with a small pile of handwritten responses; title card | Analyse/Discuss grading combines transcription, causal-chain checking, rubric matching, and feedback. |
| 0:20–0:40 | Public home screen and synthetic-data warning | EconMark is teacher-first; public mode uses only original synthetic work and creates no automatic final mark. |
| 0:40–1:05 | Choose the middle script; zoom into image/transcript | Vision preserves the student's words. The teacher corrects and confirms before any scoring call. |
| 1:05–1:25 | Question/rubric confirmation | The supplied rubric is authoritative; conflicting totals or missing levels stop the workflow. |
| 1:25–1:55 | Evidence cards and exact highlighted quotes | Every proposed credit carries a verbatim quote and rubric reference; duplicate or invented evidence fails a code check. |
| 1:55–2:15 | Primary/reviewer/adjudicated marks and confidence | An isolated second pass checks over-crediting. One-mark differences are adjudicated; larger differences require manual review. |
| 2:15–2:35 | English/Chinese tabs and two priorities | Feedback is bilingual, criterion-specific, and preserves the same two improvement priorities. It does not write a replacement essay. |
| 2:35–2:48 | Adjust mark and type reason; export JSON/PDF | The teacher approves, adjusts with a reason, or rejects. Only then can a final mark exist. |
| 2:48–3:00 | Aggregate validation chart plus reusable workflow diagram | Report real agreement/time results and show that schemas/prompts can migrate or be adapted after subject-specific validation. |
