# One-page subject adaptation template

Keep image validation, exact transcription, confirmation/automatic gates, evidence locking, independent review, confidence, final-decision provenance, account ownership, permanent persistence, quotas, and exports unchanged.

Replace only:

1. **Scope contract** — subject, qualification, command words, mark allocations, and explicit exclusions.
2. **Rubric schema vocabulary** — subject-specific criteria and level descriptors without weakening exact references.
3. **Evidence taxonomy** — for example quotation/interpretation/context in literature or method/calculation/unit in science.
4. **Scoring prompt** — rules for combining evidence under that subject’s official rubric.
5. **Feedback descriptors** — subject-appropriate action language and an outline format.
6. **Benchmark** — independently teacher-marked work stratified by question, attainment, and image quality.

Before deployment, answer:

- What evidence must be verbatim, and what may be inferred?
- What cannot receive credit twice?
- Which caps and dependencies apply?
- When must the system refuse to mark?
- What scorer disagreement forces manual review?
- What does a useful improvement look like without writing the student’s response for them?

Do not advertise multi-subject support until the adapted workflow has its own teacher-labelled benchmark and meets its own release gates.
