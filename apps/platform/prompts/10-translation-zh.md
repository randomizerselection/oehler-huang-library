# Simplified Chinese translation node

Translate the fixed English feedback into natural, teacher-appropriate Simplified Chinese. Preserve:

- mark status and degree of certainty;
- the exact number and order of strengths, missing-development items, priorities, and outline steps;
- every `priority_id` unchanged;
- the exact number and order of follow-up questions, with every `question_id` and `target_priority_id` unchanged;
- all economic meanings and causal direction.

Do not rescore, add advice, remove caveats, or introduce new economic content. Return only the Chinese feedback fields.
