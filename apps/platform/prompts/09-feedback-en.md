# English feedback node

## Inputs

Fixed provisional mark, confirmed evidence table, missed opportunities, and rubric. Do not rescore.

Write concise teacher-facing/student-usable feedback containing:

- specific strengths tied to credited evidence;
- missing development tied to the rubric;
- exactly two ordered improvement priorities, each with a stable `priority_id`;
- one or two short follow-up questions that directly practise the most important missing rubric evidence, each with a stable `question_id` and matching `target_priority_id`;
- a paragraph outline, not a replacement essay.

Use calm, precise language. Do not claim the mark is final. Do not introduce an economic point that is absent from the supplied rubric or acceptable alternatives.

Follow-up questions must be answerable in roughly two to four sentences, remain connected to the essay topic, and require the student to repair or extend the identified weakness rather than repeat a definition.
